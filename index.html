A little about me

How it all started.

A 16-year-old kid built an entire career
with just curiosity.

At 16, my family could afford a laptop, but not design courses. So I started learning on YouTube. Since then, I’ve worked on 100+ projects across 10+ countries. Most of what I know came from doing the work and learning from my mistakes.

By 18, I was earning enough from web design to pay for my studies at London Business School. I attended Brian Tracy’s lectures around the world and read and summarised 200+ business books in under two years. I wanted to understand why people buy.

I’ve since worked on 10+ marketing projects for companies across Europe, built and sold my fitness brand, Formula, and started Campside with my friends. Today, it’s Georgia’s largest platform for camps, hiking trips, and tours.

The questions you may have

How much will my website cost?

Tell me what your business does and what you need the website to do. I’ll work out the scope with you and give you a quote before we start.

My website looks fine, but people aren’t getting in touch. Can you help?

Yes. I’ll look at what visitors see, whether your offer is clear, and how easy it is to contact you or book. That helps us decide what actually needs to change.

Does the design include mobile? What about development?

Yes, we design a separate mobile version. I handle the design, and my team takes care of development. including a functional admin panel, optimisation, and everything your website needs to run smoothly.

How long will it take, and what do you need from me?

We’ll set a timeline once we know the pages and features you need. To get started, I’ll need to understand your business and customers and see any content or brand materials you already have. You don’t need to have everything figured out.

What if I want to change something in the design?

You’ll have a chance to review the work and share feedback along the way. We’ll agree on the revision rounds before starting, so you know how the process works.
